About HelpDesk

Your All-in-One
Communication Center

Today, your customers can reach you through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, or from your website. For your team, that can mean scattered conversations, lost information, and customers waiting for a reply.

HelpDesk was born to change that. We bring all your communication channels together in one place so you can manage every conversation with more order, context, and control.

Built to simplify customer service

Communicating with your customers shouldn't depend on how many apps you have open.

HelpDesk centralizes your conversations so your team can reply from one place, keep the context of each customer, and work in an organized way.

One single place to manage them all.

Why does HelpDesk exist?

Businesses started communicating with their customers through more channels. The problem is that each channel created a new inbox, a new notification, and a new way of working. That makes serving customers more complicated than it needs to be.

"Your team should adapt to your customers, not your customers to your tools."

That's why we built a platform that brings conversations together in one place and makes the daily work easier for those behind every reply.

A better way to work

HelpDesk helps your team get a complete view of their conversations.

Everything in one place

View and reply to conversations from different channels from a single inbox, without jumping between tabs.

Context when you need it

Keep each conversation's information and history available so your team can reply with greater accuracy.

Organized work

Distribute conversations, assign responsibilities to specific agents, and prevent messages from going unattended.

A consistent experience

Give your customers smooth, professional service no matter which channel they choose to contact you from.

We built HelpDesk to grow with you

Your business can start by handling just a few conversations a day and end up managing hundreds. The way you work should also be able to evolve.

That's why HelpDesk is designed to support the growth of your operations, from basic conversation organization to more structured management of teams and customers.
