Frequently Asked Questions

Which communication channels can I unify in HelpDesk?
You can connect multiple channels, including WhatsApp Business, Instagram Direct, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, Email, and our own Live Chat widget for your website. All centralized in a single inbox.
Can I keep using my current WhatsApp number?
Yes! You can keep your current WhatsApp number. We connect it officially through the WhatsApp Business API so your whole team can reply simultaneously without losing the history.
Do I need programming knowledge to create automations?
Not at all. Our interface is visual and drag-and-drop. You can build chat flows and set up automated replies or AI bots in a matter of minutes.
Do you offer a free trial period?
Yes, we offer a completely free 14-day trial. You can explore all premium features, connect your channels, and test the power of automation without needing to register a credit card.
How safe is my customers' data?
Security is our priority. We comply with the highest data protection standards (including GDPR). All information is encrypted in transit and stored on secure servers with continuous backups.
